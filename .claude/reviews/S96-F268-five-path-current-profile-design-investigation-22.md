## Counting method and limits

The table below is independently derived from each raw profile.sample.txt call graph, excluding the later flat symbol totals. Parentage follows the indentation of the numeric call-graph entries. A node's exclusive weight is its printed sample count minus its immediate children's counts. All parsed exclusive weights are nonnegative in these five reports.

Only stacks containing the actual requested binding method are included: PyDocument reply_to, PyParagraph replace_text, PyDocument replace_text_at, PyCell add_picture or PyDocument insert_paragraph. Their distinct printed program-counter branches are combined. Idle stdin waiting, setup, verification and unrelated Python frames are excluded. The denominator is therefore requested-binding-active samples, not every process sample and not complete PyO3 trampoline overhead.

For an inclusive category, an exclusive stack weight contributes once if any function in that active ancestry matches the named family. Nested functions within one category are not added repeatedly. Different categories still overlap. For example, an allocator sample can simultaneously belong to quick_xml, capture planning, a paragraph reader and comment preparation. No row or column of categories may be summed into a total or subtracted from another as an elapsed saving.

The one-millisecond sampler setting is not an exact per-function clock. These counts are not invocation counts, percentages of the ordinary benchmark, complete reader/clone counts or statistical confidence intervals. There are 90prepared documents per process and only the prescribed action interval is sampled. Physical-footprint lines include that entire diagnostic population and are not Q22 or final RSS evidence. PROFILES.json diagnostic_elapsed_s also includes driver/setup/sampling/verification work and is not API timing.
